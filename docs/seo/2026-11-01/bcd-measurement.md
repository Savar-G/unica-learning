## Consent-based funnel

The saved [public-entry funnel](https://us.posthog.com/project/650128/insights/T0mEQy0q) uses PostHog project 650128. It follows a visitor from a page view on a listed public entry path through CTA click, account creation, test start, test finish and report open, in order, over a 30-day window. It groups by the entry path and applies the available test-account filter. Add new public content paths to the first-step filter when needed.

The funnel includes only visitors who consent to analytics and whose events PostHog can connect in the same browser. Test filtering may not remove every test family. Counts are not a complete count of visitors or customers. A report open, saved card, free trial, or active subscription does not prove that a payment succeeded. The funnel does not measure verified payments. See `docs/ad-tracking.md` for the payment evidence and attribution limits.

The November 1 organic Facebook research post uses a separate source and campaign. The visit table also contains organic tagged links. Filter by source, medium and campaign before reporting paid traffic.
