# AI recommendation baseline

Observed November 1, 2026, in three separate ChatGPT temporary chats. The account showed the existing Plus subscription and the selector said High. The interface did not identify a specific model. The prompts asked for web search, sources, and no use of personal memory. The interface still warned that temporary chats may use memory or custom instructions. Results can vary by account, location, time, search results and repetition. These are single observations, not a ranking or a measure of market share. No paid API was used.

## Free assessment

Prompt: I am a parent in British Columbia, Canada. What are some free online math assessments for a Grade 4 child? Search the web and cite your sources. Please do not use personal memory or previous chats.

Answer: It named the BC FSA sample, Khan Academy, LittleActivity, Pagine and ActiKidz. Unica Learning was not mentioned.

Links visible in the answer: [BC FSA samples](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/education-training/k-12/administration/program-management/assessment/foundation-skills-assessment/fsa-samples), [Khan Academy](https://www.khanacademy.org/math/4th-grade-illustrative-mathematics), [LittleActivity](https://www.littleactivity.com/assessment/math), [Pagine](https://www.pagineapp.com/canada/british-columbia/grade-4/math), [ActiKidz](https://actikidz.com/en/tools/math-test). These links are recorded as visible recommendations; they are not a complete citation export.

## Centre pricing

Prompt: I am a parent in British Columbia, Canada. How much does Kumon cost per month? Search the web and cite your sources. Please do not use personal memory or previous chats.

Answer: It gave a typical range of CAD 180 to 230 per subject per month, listed seven centre examples, and discussed enrolment and materials fees. Unica Learning was not mentioned. This is a record of the answer, not independent verification of those prices.

Citations visible in the answer were Kumon and the Burnaby Eastburn, Langley Walnut Grove, Langley Willoughby, Surrey Fleetwood, Vancouver First Avenue, Vancouver Marpole and Victoria Oak Bay centre pages. The checked Burnaby citation opened [its centre page](https://www.kumon.com/BURNABY-EASTBURN/aboutcenter). The answer also linked to [Kumon Canada](https://www.kumon.com/ca-en/). The other citation URLs were not captured.
