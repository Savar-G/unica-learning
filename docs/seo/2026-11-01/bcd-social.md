What do learning centre fees look like in BC?

We checked the published fees at 13 BC locations of one learning centre company on November 1, 2026. They listed $180 to $260 per subject each month, plus an $80 enrolment fee. Materials fees may apply.

These are listed prices, not quotes for a child. The sample does not cover every BC centre. Each centre sets its own fees.
