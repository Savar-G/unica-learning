# Free assessment blocks

Issue #651. D-283 records the approved product rules.

New tests start at school grade. Each part has six questions. Five or six right moves up when the next grade is untested. Zero to two right moves down. Three or four right asks for more evidence at the same grade. After two parts at a grade, the cumulative 70% bar resolves the direction. A confirmed level needs at least 12 scored answers. Above-grade questions do not create a reported weakness in an area.

The initial search stops after 36 questions. Extra parts confirm the highest grade with passing evidence. When none passed, confirmation continues one grade below the lowest grade tested. It never infers Kindergarten from failures at a much higher grade. The test also fills six scored answers per reported area at or below school grade. When a province bank has fewer than six available, it uses all available questions and keeps the existing certainty labels. The absolute limit is 72 questions.

The limit can stop an unusually large upward search before the bank ceiling. The confirmed level is then a conservative starting point, not a claim that higher work failed. The test never reports a grade above the highest grade with 12 scored answers. Kindergarten is also a valid supported starting point after 12 Kindergarten answers, even when the child has not cleared them. Grades below 9 never receive Grade 9 content.

"I don't know yet" appears after 15 seconds. It is stored as that exact response for either answer format. It scores as wrong, carries no misconception, and breaks a run of fast guesses. The child can replace it while the part remains open. It does not skip the timed facts section.

The stored test holds a versioned, seeded question pool and the selected parts. A part lock compares the final responses and times under the assessment row lock. It commits the next part and the review lock together. A competing answer change either wins first and forces a new routing read, or loses because the part is locked. A lost response can retry the same part number. It cannot create a second next part. Parents receive no answer keys or future questions.

Review pages allow pause. The existing 24-hour answer window applies. The break and quick facts occur once after question 12. The screen names the current part's progress because the final length is not yet known. Existing tests retain their two or three stored stages.

The completion path requires confirmed level evidence and the area's available scored answer evidence. Answers excluded as rushed satisfy neither requirement. If the cap or the bank prevents that, it saves the answers and asks the grown-up to contact us. It sends no report and starts no workbook. A test stopped before completion also sends no result. There is no new automatic grade reduction in workbook placement.

The thresholds are initial product settings. The earlier simulation compares designs under assumptions. It does not prove placement accuracy for real children. Review unknown-answer use, completion length, area evidence and first-workbook results after real use.

Admin exports keep the whole adaptive pool. Selected questions carry their block and answer positions. Deletion copies keep the exact unknown response and its answer-change history. Other free text keeps the existing privacy filter.
