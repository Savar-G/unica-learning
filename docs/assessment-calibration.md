# Assessment calibration review

## Review template difficulty by school grade

1. Open **Quality → Templates** in admin. Turn off **Show test families**, choose one **School grade**, and apply the filters. Set **Curriculum** to the curriculum you intend to review. The school grade is the child's grade when the test started. The table sections group templates by the grade of their BC standard.
2. Open a template marked **Ready for manual review**. The detail page carries the school grade and curriculum filters over. Check each relevant school grade separately. Wait for at least 30 answered attempts for that template in each cohort you use. Use the displayed **answered** count. **Shown** also includes questions served without an answer. Attempts can include repeat tests by the same child.
3. Read percent correct, median time where available, common wrong answers, and the sample questions against the linked BC standard. Check whether the prompt, reading load, arithmetic, or answer choices explain the results. Adaptive routing affects who sees a question, so treat accuracy as evidence about the children who answered it. If the evidence is mixed or sparse, keep the current rating and revisit the cohort later.
4. If the evidence supports a change, propose the template edit for manual review. Include the template ID, filters, review date, answered count, percent correct, and reason in the review. Keep family and child details out of the proposal.

## Check placement against approved evidence

Use the following checks for placement. Keep their counts out of the template's answer total.

- **Report-card evidence.** When a school math result has already been shared and approved for this use and for recording, open the family's private contact-note form in [Admin Customer activity](customer-activity.md#optional-tracking-and-private-notes). Record the school grade, reporting term, curriculum if known, the school's math level in its original scale, and the source and approval date. Compare it with the assessment's measured level, taking the term into account. Record missing evidence as unavailable during review. Keep report-card images, full transcripts and identifying details out of calibration notes and issue discussions.
- **Early workbook marks.** In Admin Customer activity, choose Workbook 1 and open **Days and history**. Follow **Page results** for the first fully marked printed days. Compare the measured level and Workbook 1 starting level with the topics and marks on those pages. Count each original, finalized, scorable first attempt once. Calculate right out of right plus wrong, and note blanks, unscored work, unavailable pages and marks waiting for the team separately. Above 90% right on early work can flag a start that was too easy. Check the sample size, skill coverage and the workbook's intended warm-up before drawing a placement conclusion. Leave the result open when the evidence is thin.

## Invented example

A template has 35 shown and 29 answered in Grade 3 BC. It waits for review. Grade 4 BC has 30 answered and 24 correct on the same template, so that cohort can be reviewed at 80% correct. A fictional approved Grade 4 BC fall math result of “Proficient” and 23 right out of 25 scored answers on the first two Workbook 1 days are separate placement clues. The 92% workbook result warrants a look at the starting level and the skills those pages cover.
