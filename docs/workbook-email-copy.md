## Free month start, Grade 3 and above

The five-day guidance below (step 1 and the "Why 5 days?" paragraph) was approved by Savar on 2026-11-09 (variation B). It is in every variant of the plan-start email: the sibling plan email ("Welcome to Unica Learning: Alex's plan"), and the email with step 3 "Marks come later" while marking is closed.

Subject: Welcome to Unica Learning: how Alex's free month works

Hi Sam,

Thanks for joining Unica Learning. Alex's first workbook has arrived, so your free first month has started. Nothing has been charged.

As a parent, here are your responsibilities:

1. Follow the schedule, 5 days a week. The workbook has 10 days, so it takes 2 weeks. Do one day on 5 days of each week, for example Monday to Friday with the weekend off. You can choose any 5 days, but do the days in order, Day 1 to Day 10. See which pages Alex does each day: https://unicalearning.com/account/schedule. The same schedule is on page 2 of the workbook.
2. Scan the finished pages. After Alex finishes the pages, scan them: https://unicalearning.com/account/scan. Each page has its own QR code.
3. Read the marks. You will see which questions are right and wrong. On a wrong answer, press "Explain this problem" for a simple explanation that you can use with Alex.
4. Set up Maya. If Alex gets stuck, Alex can chat with Maya, our AI Teaching Assistant. Sign in at https://unicalearning.com/account, set a PIN, agree to the Maya notice, then open Alex's desk.

Why 5 days? Two days off each week give Alex time to rest, or to look back at the week's pages, and come back ready to learn. A steady 5 days builds a habit. Working every day can wear kids out and make math feel like a chore, for Alex and for you.

If anything is broken, if anything does not work, or if you have feedback, email contact@unicalearning.com or use https://unicalearning.com/account/feedback.

Your plan: On December 15, 2026, we charge CAD 79.99 to your saved card. We then charge CAD 79.99 each month until you cancel. Delivery is included. We will email you a reminder 7 days before the first charge.

To cancel, sign in and open https://unicalearning.com/account/plan, then press "Cancel plan". If you cancel before December 15, 2026, you pay nothing.

Thank you,
The Unica Learning team

## Onboarding additions (#970)

The dispatch preview above replaces the earlier promise to send instructions later.
Both dispatch and plan-start messages show `Watch the 3-minute walkthrough` near
the top only when `ONBOARDING_WALKTHROUGH_URL` is a valid HTTPS URL without
credentials. Blank or invalid configuration hides the whole line. Set the URL
when the final video is ready; the email builders read it when making the message.
Already claimed or sent messages keep their saved copy and are not resent.
Welcome and ready notices do not include the walkthrough.

The plan-start message adds the day 15 next-workbook explanation, the four habits
in `src/lib/getting-started.ts`, and a reply-or-book-call help line. The call URL
comes from `src/lib/contact.ts`; disabling booking keeps reply-by-email available.
The plan, first charge, reminder and cancellation paragraphs stay unchanged.
Maya's existing chat wording and Grade 3+ condition stay unchanged.
The welcome-guide link waits for #967.

### Personal Day 2 follow-up

`/admin/next-steps` has a daily **Day 2 check-ins today** list. Two local calendar
days after a completed initial plan start, the team can open a personal email
draft: “How did the first day go? Did scanning work?” Nothing is sent automatically.
The list groups siblings by family and follows the admin's test-family switch;
test families never count. It is independent of card-list pagination.

This is a daily contact list, not a persistent task tracker. It stays visible for
the due local day, even after the team sends a message; it does not claim that
opening a draft completes a contact. Missed contacts do not carry over. This small
manual workflow is the chosen implementation while families are few. A persistent
completion/overdue ledger can be added separately if the team needs it.
