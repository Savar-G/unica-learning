## Notes so far (partial)
### Kumon (search snippets of kumon.com centre pages; WebFetch/curl blocked 403, so numbers are via search-tool extraction of official pages, NOT directly read - mark "verify live before publishing")
- Canada centres: 403 (Kumon press release, undated, ~2023 "including 23 opened in 2022") https://www.kumonfranchise.com/ca-en/assets/pressrelease/release2023-0101.html (fetched OK). Third-party 374 (Apr 2023).
- Per-centre prices (kumon.com centre pages, per subject per month; enrollment CAD? $80/student; materials $30 at some): Vancouver Marpole $230-260; Vancouver First Ave $210; Vancouver Cedar Cottage $190-220 (page "about 2,349 days old" i.e. stale); Burnaby Highgate $190; Burnaby Heights $185-200; Toronto Danforth $195; Toronto Jane&Bloor $190; Ottawa Carling $180; Virtual (Calgary) $150, enrol $80. => varies by centre; Metro Van ~ $185-260.
- Founder's "~$200/subject" is defensible as Metro Vancouver midpoint, but say "about $190-$260 at Metro Vancouver centres; varies by centre".
### Mathnasium
- Canada count: unclear (">100 in Canada" per Dec-2025 release per search summary; third-party directory lists 25). Unverified. Vancouver-area: North Van, West Van pages exist. No official price. Official FAQ: price varies by program/area. Third-party: Peel Region ~CAD 379/mo (unverified, single parent-sourced site cuemath). Math only.
### Oxford Learning
- ~140 (CFA franchise listing) to 150+ (128 in Ontario) Canada; mostly Ontario, few BC. Prices not published. CBC ~2018: $385-420/mo for 2h/wk Toronto. Edmonton South summer 2026: 25h for $1,100 (=$44/h). Older assessment fees $100-150.
### Spirit of Math
- ~29 campuses (own claim, via search), mostly ON/AB/MB/BC; Selective, B+ students; 39 weeks; reg fee $30/yr; tuition 2024-25 HQ Toronto approx $1,079-1,365 per year for grades 1-8 (~$28-35/class-week?). Math only. Annual fee, not monthly; I cannot parse.
### Sylvan
- Vancouver (Kerrisdale, 5687 Yew St) listed on sylvanlearning.com; North Van & West Van status unclear (FB says permanently closed for North Van). 
### Tutors
- Math Tutor Network Vancouver: Math K-7 private $38/h online, $40/h in-person; group $28/$30 per student/h; "starting rates"; currency not stated (https://www.mathtutornetwork.com/rates.html, fetched 2026-10-08)
- Tutorlyft (Jun 28 2026): K-7 ~ $38 online / $40 in person; agencies from $45; overall Vancouver math $30-100. 
- Genie Teacher: Toronto & Vancouver $60-100/h. Superprof: math Vancouver typical $29/h, range up to $100.

## More findings
### Sylvan (Canada)
- Pricing not published for Kerrisdale (page says not listed online). Only dated CAD data: assessment fee up to CAN$199 (regular), cut to $29 in Apr 2021 promo (newswire.ca). US pricing pages (Vancouver WA) are USD, ignore. Franchise sets price. BC presence: Kerrisdale (5687 Yew St) confirmed on sylvanlearning.com; North/West Van status unclear. Canada count: not found. Weak candidate for a column.
### Oxford Learning
- BC official directory: ~14+ centres in Metro Van/Victoria (Burnaby 1; Surrey 3; Vancouver 5: East Van, Kerrisdale, Kitsilano, Oakridge, Wesbrook; plus N Van, W Van, Richmond, Steveston, Coquitlam, Burquitlam, Langley, Victoria). Canada ~140-150 (franchise listing, unverified date). Prices not published; individualized one-on-one/small group; assessment first. Strong local mindshare in Metro Van.
### Mathnasium
- Math only, ~1 instructor to 3-4 kids, membership + enrolment fee; no CAD price published; not usable for a price cell.
### Eye Level / JEI / RSM / Spirit of Math
- Eye Level: Vancouver (Kingsway, Oakridge), Coquitlam, Calgary, Ottawa. JEI: ON + Calgary only (no BC found). RSM: Toronto, Richmond Hill only (no BC found). Spirit of Math: ~29 campuses, selective (B+), annual fee, few BC. None are good columns for a BC audience.
### Online
- IXL: ca.ixl.com family page "as low as $12.95 CAD/month" (via search snippet; fetch failed). IXL HAS a Real-Time Diagnostic, personalized recommendations, action plan, parent analytics (blog.ixl.com 2025-02-04). So "Little adapting / Limited seeing where stuck" is NOT accurate for IXL.
- Khan Academy Kids free (ages 2-8). Prodigy: base free; paid tiers USD-like 9.95-19.95/mo per child, currency not shown. Prodigy Math/IXL both adapt.
### Kumon method (official Kumon pages via search): placement test at enrolment sets starting level; students "move through levels at their own pace"; ~30 min/subject/day, centre twice a week, rest at home; instructor reviews work and updates lesson plan; Kumon Connect tablet option + app shows worksheets, times, scores for parents; myKumon Portal parent dashboard.
- => Draft cell "Standardized: the same program for every child" is NOT defensible (Kumon markets individualized starting level & pace). Defensible: "Fixed worksheet sequence; your child's starting level is set by a placement test."
- => "Seeing where they're stuck: at the centre" is incomplete: Kumon Connect/myKumon give parents scores & times. 
- Reddit: curl returned HTML (blocked); no forum figures used.

2. Kumon prices came from search-tool extraction of kumon.com centre pages (direct fetch returned 403). Re-open Marpole, First Avenue, Burnaby Highgate pages in a browser before publishing. Cedar Cottage page is flagged stale; currency not printed on pages.
