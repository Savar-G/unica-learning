# Compare page research (accessed 2026-10-16)

For the /compare page (round r24): school, tutor programs, learning centres, online learning platforms. Adds to `competitors-2026-10.md`. "direct" = page read; "snippet" = search result only (Kumon, Mathnasium and the Prodigy help centre blocked fetches).

## BC public school
- Class size caps, School Act s.76.1: K 22, grades 1-3 24 (s.76.1(2)); grades 4-12 30 unless superintendent and principal agree, with teacher compensation above 30 (s.76.1(2.1)-(2.3)). https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/96412_06 (direct). Collective agreement limits may be lower and vary; not confirmed for 2026.
- Student Reporting Policy (effective 2023-07-01, page updated 2026-08-19): at least 5 updates a year = 2 Written Learning Updates + 1 Summary of Learning + at least 2 informal updates. K-9 uses Emerging / Developing / Proficient / Extending. Written updates include "Descriptive Feedback on areas of significant growth and opportunities for further development." https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/education-training/k-12/administration/legislation-policy/public-schools/student-reporting (direct)
- Ministry leaflet: the scale "lets you know what your child still needs to work on." https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/education/administration/kindergarten-to-grade-12/information-for-parents-and-caregivers-why-is-my-child-not-getting-letter-grades.pdf (direct)
- Curriculum is set per grade but "allows for instructional flexibility"; no mandated pace. https://curriculum.gov.bc.ca/curriculum/mathematics/introduction (direct)
- No provincial homework policy found.

## Tutor programs
- Vancouver math tutors $30-$100/h, agencies from $45, K-7 about $38 online / $40 in person (updated 2026-06-28). https://www.tutorlyft.com/blogs/how-much-does-a-math-tutor-in-vancouver-cost-per-hour (direct)
- Many companies do not publish prices (e.g. Tutorax). No authoritative typical session length. Examples: Cuemath 40 min twice a week; Thinkster 45 min 1-3 times a week.
- Arithmetic only: 1 h/week at $40-$100 = about $173-$433 a month (x 4.33).

## Learning centres
- Kumon: centre twice a week, worksheets at home other days, about 30 min per subject; free placement test; enrolment fee about $80 (Richmond West $50), materials $30 at some. BC centre pages list about $170-$220 per subject a month (Surrey Strawberry Hill $150-$310); some listings look old. (snippet) https://www.kumon.com/resources/canadian_english/what-kumon/ ; https://www.kumon.com/burnaby-metrotown/aboutcenter ; https://www.kumon.com/surrey-city-centre/aboutcenter
- Mathnasium: 2-3 visits a week, about 60 min; no published Canadian price; enrolment fee and assessment exist, amounts not published. (snippet) https://university.mathnasium.com/faq
- Oxford Learning: no published price; example two 1-hour classes a week; 3 students per teacher. https://oxfordlearning.com/tutoring-faqs (direct)
- Sylvan: no Canadian price found.

## Online learning platforms
- IXL Canada: single subject C$12.95, combo C$19.95, all access C$24.95 (monthly per earlier research). Diagnostic and personalized plan. https://ca.ixl.com/membership/family/pricing ; https://ca.ixl.com/diagnostic (direct)
- Prodigy: free content; Core $9.95, Plus $14.95, Ultra $19.95 a month in USD (converted on Canadian cards); parent progress tracking. https://www.prodigygame.com/Memberships/math/ (direct)
- Khan Academy Kids free (ages 2-8); Khan Academy free. https://www.khanacademy.org/kids (direct)
- Mathletics Canada: $99/yr one student; weekly emailed parent report. https://www.mathletics.com/ca/?p=155 (direct)
- Live online tutoring (Cuemath CA$17-$32.50 per class plus platform fee; Thinkster from $22.50-$45 per session, likely USD) is closer to tutor programs than platforms.
- Canadian Paediatric Society (2019, reaffirmed 2025-07-04): no daily hour limit for ages 5-12; "4 Ms"; avoid screens 1 hour before bed. https://cps.ca/en/documents/position/digital-media (direct)

## Not defensible
- School report cards "don't say what to work on" (policy requires descriptive feedback).
- "30+ kids per class"; "BC sets a fixed pace"; any BC homework rule.
- Screens "bad for learning" or a CPS hour limit for this age.
- Current prices for Mathnasium, Oxford Learning, Sylvan.
- Prodigy prices as CAD.
- Online platforms "don't adapt" or "don't report to parents".
- $100/h as a typical tutor rate.
