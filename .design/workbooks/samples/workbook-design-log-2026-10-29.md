## 1. Decisions made

| Topic | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Size | **Letter, 8.5 × 11 in** (Figma pages 816 × 1056, 64 px padding) | Matches most retail workbooks, the in-house printer, the pricing model and the $4.29 postage band (stay under about 200 g). Bigger "textbook" sizes need special paper, weigh more and are harder to scan. Not Kumon's small centre sheets. |
| Colour | **Colour inside** (decided Oct 27, 2026; V5 recoloured in place) | One colour per block type so a child can find their way around a page: New idea blue #3366F0 (labels #2453D4 on #EAF0FF), Practice green #16A06A, Word problems tangerine #FF6A3D, Review yellow #FFD23F, Challenge chip #FFF0B8 with dark border and text (changed Oct 29, 2026 in the accessibility pass; was #2B2622 with #FFD23F text). Colour print cost still to quote; the earlier black-and-white decision was about cost only. |
| Answer key | **None in the book** | Stops copying. The app marks scanned pages and shows the solutions. A separate parent-only sheet is possible later. |
| QR codes | Removed in the first round, then **added back to every page footer** in V2 with the page ID (e.g. UL·LEAH·W03·D04·P1) | Cedric's sticky note: pages need to be easy for the app to identify. The QR codes in Figma are placeholders and don't scan yet. |
| Scan prompt | **No "take a photo of this page" line** on every page | Parents know by then. |
| Corner marks | Kept on every page | Help the app straighten a photo. |
| Answer format | **Every answer goes in a box**, including challenge, review and word problems | Easier for the app to find and mark. |
| Work space | **Plain empty boxes**, no dots | Cedric's note. |
| Daily check-in and start/end time | Removed from the paper workbook for now (Oct 28, 2026). The space goes to work boxes; nothing replaces it yet. | Decided Oct 28, 2026. Revisit after first families. |
| Step numbers | **Removed** ("Find: / Plan: / Solve / Check", no 1, 2, 3) | Looked crowded. |
| Level names | **U1 to U30** ("Level U12"; "U12" alone where space is tight) | Simple and branded; carries no grade. Decided Oct 27, 2026. |
| Context and flexible strategies | Decided Oct 29, 2026 (prompted by a high-school math department head). **Context grows with the level:** Levels U1-U9 one-step picture stories; U10-U18 two-step stories where the child chooses the operation (mixed set: "Operation:" tick boxes on the left, "Answer" and answer box on the right, "Show your work" below); U19 and up a weekly **"Math in the world"** page on Days 4 and 9, replacing one practice page (pages a day unchanged): one longer multi-step real-world task with room to plan (budgets, phone plans, recipe scaling, map scales, sports stats, BC contexts such as ferry fares or ski passes). Every level test has at least one context question. **Flexible strategies are a visible thread:** New idea pages show the worked example solved two ways (49 + 26 as 50 + 25, or 40 + 20 + 9 + 6); one "Solve it two ways" item a day on a practice page; one mental-math warm-up item with a "How did you work it out?" line. | BC competencies: model with mathematics in situational contexts; flexible computation and mental math strategies. Passing a level should mean applying the skill, not only computing it. Strategy tags on question templates are not built yet. |
| Answer choices | **Tick boxes**: small squares tight to their label, never pills. Every instruction names the choices ("Tick yes or no.", "Tick prime, composite or neither."). Mixed set layout: question line; "Operation:" tick boxes on the left and "Answer" with an answer box on the right; "Show your work" below. Instruction: "Tick the operation: + or −. Then write the answer in the Answer box." | Decided Oct 29, 2026. |
| Fix it and corrections | Fix it **carries over between workbooks**, not from yesterday. Workbook 1 has none. From workbook 2 the Fix it box (page 1, Days 1–5) holds **new questions with fresh numbers** on skills the child got wrong in the previous workbook (up to the day-7 snapshot; errors the child fixed in the app don't carry over). Spare room becomes spaced review. The box shows only a "FIX IT" chip, no explanation line. Parent line: "Anything your child got wrong last time comes back as new questions." Corrections order: the parent scans after the day's pages are done; the app shows only which answers are wrong; the child tries to fix them first; then the Teaching Assistant explains; the first try is the score. | Decided Oct 29, 2026. |
| Stuck cue and footer | The "Stuck? Scan this page and call the Teaching Assistant" cue and the "Scan this page in the Unica app" footer line are removed from day pages. Families learn scanning and the Teaching Assistant once, at onboarding and on the How + Plan page. Footer = QR code + page ID. | Decided Oct 29, 2026. |
| Accessibility pass | Page headers are plain coloured text (no pill) in the page type's dark colour. Chips have dark text on tinted fills. Challenge chip is #FFF0B8 with dark border and text. Child-facing text ≥ 13 px. Answer boxes ≥ 40 px. Writing lines ≥ 34 px apart. | Decided Oct 29, 2026. |

## 2. Workbook structure (one two-week workbook)

- **10 practice days**, 5 a week, about 15–30 minutes a day (a typical target, not a limit; a day can run over).
- **Pages a day by band** (curriculum skeleton, Oct 27, 2026; the pages-versus-minutes question stays open until children are timed on real pages): Levels U1–U3: 4–6 short, parent-led pages; Levels U4–U9: 10–12; Levels U10–U18: 8–10; Levels U19–U30: 6. V5 (Level U10, Leah) uses **8 pages a day** at about 27–28 minutes. The old "46–60 sides a book" estimate in the pricing notes predates this.
- Kumon's principles used as internal inspiration only (never named in customer copy): very small steps, one skill per page, clean layout with lots of white space, a guided start, and a marker for harder problems.
- Review of older skills grows with the level: about 10% of pages in Levels U1–U3, 25% around Level U10, 30–35% from Level U19 (see the skeleton's Workbook format tab).
- No printed "Stuck?" cue on day pages (removed Oct 29, 2026; the How + Plan page explains scanning and the Teaching Assistant once). From workbook 2, Days 1–5 carry a **"Fix it" box** on page 1 with new questions on last workbook's wrong skills (see section 1). V7 still shows the old Fix it on page 1 from Day 2 and the Stuck cue in each New idea box.

**Current page set (V3):**
1. **Cover:** "Made for Leah · Grade 3 math" (large and bold), the logo grid at about 40% of its original size, and a box labelled **"Made from Leah's assessment"** saying what the assessment showed and what this workbook does about it. The parent-only "For you, the grown-up" label and the "20 minutes a day / scan" line were dropped. The cover says "Your day-by-day plan is on the next page."
2. **How + Plan page:** explains scanning and the Teaching Assistant once; all 10 days, each with its topic, why it's in Leah's plan (e.g. "Where you slowed down on your assessment"), an example problem and a done box.
3. **Days 1–10.**

**Each day page, in order:**
- Header: Day, Name, Date.
- **"What's going on":** on days that introduce something new, a short paragraph explaining the idea first (e.g. 52 is 5 tens and 2 ones; borrowing trades 1 ten for 10 ones without changing the number).
- Worked example as short steps, shown solved two ways on New idea days (e.g. Day 4: look at the ones, borrow, subtract the ones, subtract the tens).
- Try it / practice: problems get harder within the day ("They get harder as you go").
- Challenge and review.
- Footer: QR code and page ID only (no scan line), plus corner marks.
