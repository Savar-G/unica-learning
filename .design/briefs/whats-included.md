# What's included page - structure draft

Status: draft for Savar's review (2026-10-29). Decisions: D-170 to D-173. Copy below is working copy, not final.

## Job

**Readers:** (1) a parent on the site who wants to see what they pay for, from pricing, How it works or the nav; (2) a parent who gets the link in outreach and has never seen Unica.

**Job:** In one scroll, see every part of Unica in the order a family meets it, and see who does what. Then take the free assessment.

**Spine:** follow one child, Leah, through her first month. Her stuck question (Day 4, page 2, question 11: 300 - 128) appears in the workbook, the AI Teaching Assistant, the app and the dashboard, so each chapter leads into the next one. All screens come from the Figma board "Personalized Learning, Delivered".

## Chapters

Each chapter has an anchor link (for outreach), a "who and how long" tag, and one interactive part.

0. **Intro and chapter map.** Kicker "What's included". Working title: "Everything your child gets, in the order it happens." Lead: "Follow Leah through her first month of Unica." A chapter map with six stops (Assessment, Workbook, AI Teaching Assistant, Unica app, Dashboard, Next workbook) that stays in view while the page scrolls and shows where the reader is. Button: "Take free assessment".

1. **Take the free assessment** (`#assessment`; child, on a laptop or tablet). The program starts here. Shows the level band and ruler from Leah's sample report. Links: "See Leah's sample report" and "Take free assessment". Line: "You choose a plan after the report."

2. **The workbook arrives** (`#workbook`; child, 15-30 minutes a day, 5 days a week). Interactive: a workbook to flip through (cover, 10-day plan, day pages) with a level switch: Leah, Level U10 and Mateo, Level U19 (D-173). Callouts name the parts: "Made for Leah" box, the plan, the page types (warm-up, learn, practice, stories, review, challenge). Facts: 2 workbooks a month, mailed home. Phones: one page at a time, swipe, tap to zoom.
   - Hand-off beat: "Day 4, page 2. Question 11: 300 - 128. Leah is stuck."

3. **Stuck? Work it out with Maya** (`#teaching-assistant`; child, a few minutes). Interactive: a replay on a laptop: Leah's desk, Maya opens question 11, the shared whiteboard fills step by step with captions, "Your turn", Leah answers, "Write 172 on your page", the note to the parent. Play, pause, step dots. Points: it knows the page and question; it explains and draws each step; it asks, it does not just tell; the child finishes on paper; the parent gets a note. Best on a laptop or tablet, also in the app. Link to the accuracy answer (D-150). Phones: the whiteboard only.
   - Description options (pick one):
     - A: "Maya, the AI Teaching Assistant, opens your child's question on a whiteboard they both see. Maya explains each step out loud and draws it. Your child answers, asks why, or picks up the pen." (recommended)
     - B: "Your child and Maya work on the same whiteboard. Maya talks through each step and draws it. Your child does the thinking."
     - C: "Like working next to a teacher at a whiteboard. Maya draws and explains each step, and your child answers along the way."

4. **Scan the pages: the Unica app** (`#app`; parent, about a minute). Interactive: a phone plays the flow from the Figma screens: Today, Scan, Marked page, See the steps. Captions per screen; tap to pause; step dots. Points: marked like a teacher's pen; shows where it went wrong and why; worked steps. Reward points stay a small detail.

5. **See progress: the parent dashboard** (`#dashboard`; parent, any time). Interactive: a laptop with clickable tabs from the Figma dashboard (Overview, Skill map, Progress over time, What we've learned, Work history, calls with Maya, Next workbook, Account), with the "Sample data" label. Line: "The same account in the app and on the website." Phones: tabs become a row of chips, one card at a time.

6. **The next workbook** (`#next-workbook`). "Built from this week's scans": more borrowing across zero, new: multiplying by 10, less: carrying, review: unit fractions. It arrives before the current one runs out; pause or skip. A loop mark back to chapter 2.

7. **Your month at a glance.** One recap table, for the "what is the whole process?" question: who does what (child, parent, Unica), how long, and where (paper, app, website).

8. **Price and start.** "All of this: CAD 79.99 a month, first month free." The included list from the pricing card. Buttons: "Take free assessment", "See a sample report".

9. **Short FAQ.** Which devices do we need? Is Maya always right? (D-150) How much screen time is it? Do I need to know the math? What if we miss a day?

10. **Footer** (the shared footer card).
