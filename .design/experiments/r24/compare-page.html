One by one

vs School

Unica works alongside school, not instead of it.

School teaches the whole class. Unica gives your child practice at home that is made for them alone.

School is good at

  • A trained teacher, every school day
  • Friends, group work and every subject
  • Free at public schools

Harder at school

  • One teacher for up to 24 children in grades 1-3 and up to 30 in grades 4-7, so the class moves on together
  • Written report cards come 3 times a year

With Unica

  • Books follow the BC curriculum, so home practice matches class
  • More practice on the gaps, and new topics before the class gets there
  • You see the gaps after every check-in
vs Tutor programs

A tutor explains. Unica keeps them practising every day.

Tutoring is time with a person. Unica is a plan your child works through at home, 15-30 minutes a day.

A tutor is good at

  • Explaining live and answering questions
  • Adjusting to your child each session
  • Help with homework and tests

Harder with a tutor

  • Often an hour or two a week; the other days are up to you
  • $40-$100 an hour, every week
  • How much you hear about gaps depends on the tutor

With Unica

  • Practice 5 days a week, built from their last book
  • A report and worked solutions after every check-in
  • CAD 99 a month. You can keep a tutor too.
vs Learning centres

Structured like a learning centre, without the drive.

A centre gives your child a program and a routine. Unica does too, and builds each book from how they did on the last one.

A centre is good at

  • A set program and a daily habit
  • A placement test to start
  • Instructors at the centre

Harder with a centre

  • You drive there, often twice a week, and wait
  • Your child moves along a set path
  • $200+ per subject a month, often plus an enrolment fee

With Unica

  • Every book is built from their own work
  • It comes to your door
  • CAD 99 a month, no enrolment fee
vs Online learning platforms

Adapts like an online platform, on paper instead of a screen.

Online platforms adapt to every tap. Unica adapts too, from the work your child does with a pencil.

An online platform is good at

  • Adapting to answers right away
  • Low cost, and some are free
  • Practice anywhere, any time

Harder with an online platform

  • Practice happens on a screen
  • Answers are tapped or typed, so the working on paper is never marked

With Unica

  • Your child works with pencil and paper
  • You take a photo; we mark the work and show you the gaps
  • The screen stays with you
