# Child deletion wording for approval

Status: proposed. Public wording approval and dashboard release approval remain pending.

## Privacy and terms

The following paragraph is identical on both pages.

If your account page offers it, you can delete one child with a one-time code that we email to your account address. This stops that child's workbook plans at once, with no further charges and no refund for time already paid. It removes that child's data and files, and keeps a copy of each test without names as described here. Your account and other children stay, even if you delete your last child. We keep a record of deleted file locations to prevent them from being saved again. We also remove stored copies of family emails that include the child. Emails you already received stay with you. We keep email delivery records and a record of which children each email included to prevent deleted copies from being saved again. We remove these records when your family account is deleted.

## Confirmation

The child form adds this item under "What stays":

We keep email delivery records and a record of which children each email included to prevent deleted copies from being saved again. We remove these records when your family account is deleted.

## Child code email

Subject: Your code to delete one child from Unica Learning

Preview: Enter this code only if you asked to delete one child.

Hello,

Someone signed in to your account and asked to delete one child. Enter this code on that child's delete form to confirm it:

[one-time code]

The code ends in 10 minutes and works once. This stops that child's workbook plan and removes that child's data. Your account and other children stay. You cannot undo this.

If you did not ask for this, do not enter the code and do not give it to anyone. Someone else may be signed in to your account, so change your password now and email contact@unicalearning.com.

Footer: You get this email because someone asked to delete one child on your account page.

The email retains the existing Unica Learning signature and footer layout. It names no child and includes no account link.

The dashboard stays behind its release check. Approval of this wording does not enable the dashboard for all families.
