FAQ

Questions, Answered.

Is there any screen time?

Your child works on paper. The only screen is your phone camera, for the few seconds it takes to snap the finished pages.

Who is it for?

Children in grades 3 to 5 (about ages 8 to 11), in math, reading or both.

Does it follow the BC curriculum?

Yes. Every book follows the BC curriculum for your child's grade, and starts from where your child actually is.

What if we miss a few days?

The next book picks up where your child is. Nothing is lost.

What if we forget to send photos?

We send a reminder. Until new pages arrive, the next book reviews what your child last worked on.

How is the work checked?

Every page you photograph is checked, working included. The results update your child's learning profile and your progress report.

What happens to my child's work?

We use it only to make your child's next book. We never sell or share it, and you can ask us to delete it at any time.

Can we cancel?

Yes, anytime, in two clicks. You are charged only when a workbook ships.

Where do you deliver?

Metro Vancouver for now. Enter your postal code when you sign up and we'll tell you right away.
