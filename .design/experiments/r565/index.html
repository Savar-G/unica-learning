Scan pages: batch and one page

Where does the parent stay between two photos, so that 10 to 20 pages in a sitting are comfortable with one hand?

Recommendation

A, with two parts of the others: B's phone camera as the fallback when the browser refuses or the camera test fails, and C's 'No photo yet: page 2' line in the stack. Build the shared stack on the current file input first, because that path is already built and ships without A's risk. Then run the real-phone camera test, then add A.

Option A

Camera stays open Recommended

Bet
The parent stays in a live camera in the page. It reads each page's QR code and four corners, takes the photo with no tap when the page is new and still, and shows a pile. Turn the page, and it goes on.
Benefit
No tap per page. Guidance arrives while the page is in front of the camera. It cannot take the same page twice, and it does not photograph a wrong or marked page.
Cost
Needs camera permission and a browser camera build. A late refusal asks the parent to turn back, or waits to the end.
Failure risk
Unproven: a video frame may read worse than a real photo. Nobody has tested this on a real phone (#454, G13).

Option B

Stack first

Bet
The stack is home. Each photo uses the phone's own camera (what S1 already does), then returns to the stack.
Benefit
Works today on every phone. Best photo quality. No permission. Also allows choosing many photos from the phone's library.
Cost
We cannot draw on the phone's camera, so no guidance until after the photo. Every refusal arrives after the page is turned.
Failure risk
About 3 taps a page, 60 for 20 pages. Parents may stop part way. A phone short of memory can reload the page when its camera returns.

Option C

Day checklist

Bet
The day's printed pages are the home: 8 slots a day, filled as photos arrive. The parent sees at once what has no photo.
Benefit
Matches how days work (D-213). Gaps are obvious, and that matters for the Day 7 snapshot. The printed page shows what to photograph.
Cost
One tap per page, and a day switch every 8 pages. Pages out of order need more thought.
Failure risk
Empty slots can read as a list of things the child did not do. It needs the live camera too, so it has A's risk with fewer of A's benefits.

What the round found

  • A book opens as two pages, and calibration refuses two sheets (D-252). So every option has to teach 'fold the book back'. The tip, the live message and the refusal all say it.
  • The current limit of 20 photos per 10 minutes is exactly the size of the sitting Savar asks for, and retakes count. A 20-page sitting with one retake gets refused. 2 uploads at once also means the browser must queue photos in a fast loop.
  • Checks finish after the page is turned, so a refusal must not interrupt. The stack puts refused pages first; the camera shows a quiet message with 'Take it again now'.
  • A retake cannot change a finalized mark (#457). So a photo of a marked page says: 'A new photo does not change the marks', and points to Change this mark.
  • A pending Please check means the page is not complete (#457), so its row says 'Please check 1', not a score.
  • The page's QR code, read by the phone's normal camera, opens the desk link (/tutor/p/<token>), not Scan pages. A parent can do this by mistake. That screen may need a 'Scan this page' link. Not in this round.
  • Public copy (DESIGN.md How it works step 5, For parents) still says 'the Unica app', though D-193 made scanning part of the web app.

Your decisions

  • Pick the option or a mix A with B's fallback and C's gap line, in the build order above
  • Auto-take on by default, or shutter only? Auto on, with a switch at the top right. It is the only way the other hand stays on the book
  • Can Send go while some pages are refused or still being checked? Yes. Send what is ready; the others stay in the stack. Your ask reads 'send them all', so this needs your word
  • Raise the limits? Yes: 40 photos per 10 minutes, keep 60 a day, and queue in the browser. Needs a small migration
  • Tell the parent when marks are ready? One email per send, only when the parent left the page. Not in the spec, and the mock promises nothing beyond 'the marks show here'
  • Shorter child row on Scan pages for phones (one line, with Open the desk as a link)? Yes. The full block takes about 180 px above the stack. It is an exception to the r544 frame
  • Desktop: show the phone's pages live beside the QR code, with Send there too? Yes. It extends D-246. Retakes stay on the phone
  • Allow two facing pages in one photo later? Not now. It would halve the photos, but D-252 refuses two sheets to protect the crops

Not tested

  • No real phone camera: the viewfinder is drawn
  • No parent has tried any option
  • Live QR reading and auto-take are not built
  • Keyboard use is not tested end to end in the mockups
  • Targets under 44 px, measured on 8 phone states: the wordmark (19 px, from the frame), Open the desk in the short child row (32 px), and the inline links More about photos and Delete this photo (20 px).
