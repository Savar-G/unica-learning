Option A

Camera stays open Recommended

Bet
The parent stays in a live camera in the page. It reads each page's QR code and four corners, takes the photo with no tap when the page is new and still, and shows a pile. Turn the page, and it goes on.
Benefit
No tap per page. Guidance arrives while the page is in front of the camera. It cannot take the same page twice, and it does not photograph a wrong or marked page.
Cost
Needs camera permission and a browser camera build. A late refusal asks the parent to turn back, or waits to the end.
Failure risk
Unproven: a video frame may read worse than a real photo. Nobody has tested this on a real phone (#454, G13).

What the round found

  • A book opens as two pages, and calibration refuses two sheets (D-252). So every option has to teach 'fold the book back'. The tip, the live message and the refusal all say it.
  • The current limit of 20 photos per 10 minutes is exactly the size of the sitting Savar asks for, and retakes count. A 20-page sitting with one retake gets refused. 2 uploads at once also means the browser must queue photos in a fast loop.
  • Checks finish after the page is turned, so a refusal must not interrupt. The stack puts refused pages first; the camera shows a quiet message with 'Take it again now'.
  • A retake cannot change a finalized mark (#457). So a photo of a marked page says: 'A new photo does not change the marks', and points to Change this mark.
  • A pending Please check means the page is not complete (#457), so its row says 'Please check 1', not a score.
  • The page's QR code, read by the phone's normal camera, opens the desk link (/tutor/p/<token>), not Scan pages. A parent can do this by mistake. That screen may need a 'Scan this page' link. Not in this round.
  • Public copy (DESIGN.md How it works step 5, For parents) still says 'the Unica app', though D-193 made scanning part of the web app.

Savar's answers

  • Pick the option or a mix A's start screen and live camera, Choose photos and the phone's camera on the same screen, B's stack (D-269)
  • Auto-take on by default, or shutter only? Yes: on by default, with a switch (D-269)
  • Can Send go while some pages are refused or still being checked? Yes: those pages stay in the stack (D-270)
  • Raise the limits? Yes: 40 per 10 minutes, 60 a day stays (D-271)
  • Tell the parent when marks are ready? Yes: one per send, only when the parent left the page (D-272)
  • Shorter child row on Scan pages for phones (one line, with Open the desk as a link)? Yes (D-274)
  • Desktop: show the phone's pages live beside the QR code, with Send there too? Yes, with Send there (D-273)
  • Allow two facing pages in one photo later? No: Fold the book back (D-275)

Notes for the build

  • The page's QR code, read by the phone's normal camera, opens the desk link (/tutor/p/<token>), not Scan pages. The build must route it, so a parent who scans a page that way can reach Scan pages.
  • Public copy still says 'the Unica app', though D-193 made scanning part of the web app: src/components/how-it-works.tsx, how-demos.tsx, whats-included.tsx, whats-included-scan.tsx, whats-included-dash.tsx, src/app/(site)/whats-included/page.tsx, src/lib/faq.ts, src/lib/parent.ts, src/lib/ai-summary.ts, and DESIGN.md (How it works step 5, For parents, Companion app).
  • Choose photos uses a file input with multiple and no capture. The current input has capture='environment', which forces the camera; Use my phone's camera keeps it.
  • The browser queues uploads, 2 at once (concurrent_uploads). The limit change needs a migration, and the current client refuses a busy upload.
  • The email needs a new kind and a way to know that the parent left the page. It carries no child work, as the other parent emails do.
