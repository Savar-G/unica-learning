AI Teaching Assistant

Stuck? Work it out with Maya.

Maya, the AI Teaching Assistant, opens your child's question on a whiteboard they both see. Maya explains each step out loud and draws it. Your child answers, asks why, or picks up the pen.

Shown without sound. The captions show what Maya says.

  • On their exact questionMaya knows the workbook page and the question your child is on.
  • Every step on the boardEach step is drawn on the whiteboard while Maya explains it.
  • Your child does the thinkingMaya asks, your child answers, then finishes the question on paper.
Unica app

Scan the pages. The app marks them.

When your child finishes a day, take a photo of each page in the Unica app. Each page comes back marked, with the steps for anything wrong.
