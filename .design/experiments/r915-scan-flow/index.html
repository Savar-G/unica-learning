Step 4 · Done

With marks: the flow ends on the payoff: a score per page that fills in as marking finishes, tap a page to see its answers, and an Undo for a few minutes. Without: the flow ends at "sent", and marks live on a separate page.

Step 4 · Done

Good pages go to marking by themselves. A page that needs another photo shows your photo next to a good one, with the problem circled, and one button. That's the only time the parent reads anything.

Design decisions
  • Camera first. Scan pages opens a keep-shooting camera, because checking each photo while the book is open avoids coming back later. "Choose from your photos" stays one tap away for parents who already took them.
  • One or two pages per photo. The tip says two pages only once spreads mark as accurately as single pages on real photos.
  • No Send button. A page that passes the check goes to marking on its own.
  • Tips once. Shown on the first visit, then only as a picture when a photo has a problem.
