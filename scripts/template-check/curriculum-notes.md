# Primary-source notes for the offline checker

Checked 2026-10-11. These notes describe sources, not answer keys. Continue to solve every example independently. A later province tag is permitted when the primary outcome is uncertain. Report that uncertainty as a review. Do not claim high confidence from a recalled outcome. No personal child records belong in this tool.

## British Columbia

The local curriculum map lists the official content and elaborations. Grade 4 includes decimals to hundredths, perimeter, line symmetry and many-to-one graphs. Grade 5 includes equivalent fractions, thousandths, elapsed time, area/perimeter relationships and transformations. Grade 6 includes factors/multiples, order of operations, mixed numbers, ratios, percents, decimal multiplication/division, angles, volume and probability.

Sources: https://curriculum.gov.bc.ca/curriculum/mathematics/4/core, https://curriculum.gov.bc.ca/curriculum/mathematics/5/core, https://curriculum.gov.bc.ca/curriculum/mathematics/6/core.

## Ontario 2020, January 2021 edition

The official PDF is the source. Printed pages differ from PDF page numbers by one.

- Grade 2 C2.2: unknown addition/subtraction equations to 100.
- Grade 3 C2.3: unknown addition/subtraction relationships to 1000.
- Grade 4 B1.7-B1.9: decimal tenths and their fraction equivalents, not hundredths. Grade 5 B1.4: decimal hundredths. Grade 6 B1.3: decimal thousandths.
- Grade 4 E2.1: capacity in litres and millilitres. It is incorrect to attribute this expectation to Grade 3.
- Grade 4 C1: extend patterns and find missing terms.
- Grade 4 E1.2-E1.3: first-quadrant coordinates, translations and reflections.
- Grade 4 E2.3: elapsed time. E2.5-E2.7: rectangle area, missing dimensions and area/perimeter relationships.
- Grade 5 E2.4: measure and construct angles through 180 degrees.
- The exact introduction of generic polygon-base prism/pyramid face counts remains uncertain. Do not infer a specific grade from a general shape-classification expectation alone.

Source: https://assets-us-01.kc-usercontent.com/fbd574c4-da36-0066-a0c5-849ffb2de96e/3ed76499-9df4-4524-8cc2-3c0010a51811/The%20Ontario%20Curriculum%20Grades%201%E2%80%938%20-%20Mathematics%2C%202020%20%28January%202021%29.pdf

## Saskatchewan

N4.7 represents decimal tenths/hundredths and relates them to fractions. N5.6 explicitly compares/orders decimals to thousandths. N5.5 includes equivalent fractions. N5.7 includes decimal addition/subtraction to thousandths. SS3.5 includes polygon perimeter. SS5.4 includes litres/millilitres. SS5.7 includes transformations. SP5.2 includes double bar graphs. The exact earliest grade for decimal comparison is not settled by the Grade 4 representation heading alone.

Sources: https://curriculum.gov.sk.ca/CurriculumOutcomeContent?id=148&oc=79225, https://curriculum.gov.sk.ca/CurriculumOutcomeContent?id=149, https://curriculum.gov.sk.ca/CurriculumOutcomeContent?id=147.

## Manitoba

Grade 3 includes perimeter. Grade 4 includes decimal representation, fraction/decimal relationships and addition/subtraction to hundredths. Grade 5 5.N.10 explicitly compares/orders decimal tenths, hundredths and thousandths. This does not prove that comparison is a named Grade 4 outcome.

Sources: https://www.edu.gov.mb.ca/k12/framework/english/math/math_gr3.html, https://www.edu.gov.mb.ca/k12/framework/english/math/math_gr4.html, https://www.edu.gov.mb.ca/k12/framework/english/math/math_gr5.html.

## Alberta

The current official snapshot confirms time in Grade 3, decimal addition/subtraction and rectangle area in Grade 4, and million-range operations, algebraic expressions and symmetry in Grade 5. The old WNCP program is not the current K-6 program. The precise outcomes for several other tags remain unverified. Flag doubts for source review. Do not use the 2018 or 2021 draft as the implemented curriculum.

Source: https://www.alberta.ca/curriculum-mathematics (updated June 2026).
