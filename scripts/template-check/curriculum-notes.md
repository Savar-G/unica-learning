## British Columbia

The local curriculum map lists the official content and elaborations. Grade 4 includes decimals to hundredths, perimeter, line symmetry and many-to-one graphs. Grade 5 includes equivalent fractions, thousandths, elapsed time, area/perimeter relationships and transformations. Grade 6 includes factors/multiples, order of operations, mixed numbers, ratios, percents, decimal multiplication/division, angles, volume and probability.
